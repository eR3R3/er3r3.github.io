- **Engine:** Unity + VR hardware
- **Made at:** Carnegie Mellon University, National High School Game Academy, summer 2026
- **My role:** Gameplay programmer; later producer

### Overview

Elkwood is a first-person VR horror game set in a forest. A creature stalks the player — you hear it before you see it. Most of the design problem was pacing dread in VR: what makes a player genuinely tense in a headset, and what just makes them disoriented or motion-sick.

### My contributions

- VR interaction and camera handling in Unity: tuning head-tracking responsiveness so the creature could feel *behind* you without inducing nausea.
- Audio-driven tension: the creature announces itself through directional footsteps and breathing before it enters your sight.
- Scene design and lighting choices to control what the player sees at any given moment. Most of the fear-work is what you *can't* see.
