- **Role:** Lead developer. Part of my work as president of PHCS (Pinghe Computer Science Club, 130 members)
- **Scale:** 700+ users, 100+ clubs
- **Live:** [casphere.net](https://casphere.net)

### Overview

Casphere is the official CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service) platform at Shanghai Pinghe School. Students use it to log activities, request supervisor sign-off, and submit reflection evidence that counts toward their IB CAS requirement. Club leaders use it to manage members and events. The administration uses it as the authoritative record.

### Significance

It's software that **became part of someone else's everyday work.** It replaced an older process and became something the school now relies on. I started it as a club project, iterated on it with real users, and watched teachers, students, and administrators integrate it into how CAS actually happens at Pinghe.

### Related work

As PHCS president I also run a weekly session on the fundamentals of AI research — linear regression, convolutional neural networks, image classification — so that newer members have a concrete entry point into the kind of work I do. I'm also launching a school-facing hackathon that sources real technical problems from staff and clubs.
