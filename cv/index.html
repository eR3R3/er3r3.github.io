Publications

  • 2026
    CaRF: Camera-Aware Cross-View Alignment for Referring 3D Gaussian Splatting Segmentation
    ICME 2026 (Oral, Top 5%)

    Teaches a 3D-Gaussian segmentation model with paired views and camera poses, improving mIoU on Ref-LERF from 25.0 to 29.2 (+16.8% relative) over ReferSplat.

  • 2026
    Geometry-Aware Cross-Modal Graph Alignment for Referring Segmentation in 3D Gaussian Splatting
    CVPR 2026 (Poster)

    Combines semantic–spatial graphs derived from text with geometric graphs built from 3D Gaussian Splatting representations to improve referring segmentation accuracy via cross-modal alignment. mIoU improvements: +28.8% on Ref-LERF, +2.6% on LERF-OVS, +3.1% on 3D-OVS.
