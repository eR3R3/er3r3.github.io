I am **Yuwen "Lucas" Tao** (陶誉文). I am a senior at **Shanghai Pinghe School**. I love learning and thinking — and more than anything, I love turning an interest into something I can build or write about.

My current focus is **physical AI** and **3D scene understanding** — teaching a machine to connect what it sees with what a person actually means. I am the sole first author of two papers on language-guided 3D Gaussian Splatting segmentation:
- **CaRF** — [ICME 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03992), oral presentation (top 5%)
- **GeoCGA** — CVPR 2026, poster

Outside research, I lead **[PHCS](/projects/)**, our school's Computer Science Club, and built **Casphere** — the school's official CAS platform (700+ users, 100+ clubs). I also program for **FRC 6941 IronPulse Robotics** (REEFSCAPE 2025, Houston Championship) and spent the summer of 2026 at **Carnegie Mellon's National High School Game Academy** as a programmer and later producer.

I am also:
- A **2026 Regeneron ISEF Finalist** (Technology Enhances the Arts, project CaRF)
- An **Outstanding Student** at the National Talent Program (英才计划) with Prof. Xin Tan at ECNU
- A **Research Intern** at Tsinghua with Dr. Kanglei Zhou
- **USACO Platinum**, **Kaggle Competition Master**, AMC/AIME, BMT Gold

I like **distributed systems, databases, parallel programming, and GPU kernels** as much as I like 3D vision — I'm still figuring out which I want to do most. On the side I draw, play basketball, and reread Chinese fantasy novels (玄幻小说), especially *择天记*.
