ShuMath

An online platform for math and programming lessons aimed at international middle- and high-school students. We built it because the gap between “doing well at a specific contest” and “actually understanding the technique” is wider than contest culture tends to admit — and a lot of students in that gap don’t have local resources to close it. Visit at shumath.com.

Takeaways from teaching

Explaining a proof out loud is the fastest way to find out whether I actually understand it. When I think I know a step but can’t explain why that step comes next, the gap is real. Making these videos has been one of the clearest tools I have for checking my own thinking.
